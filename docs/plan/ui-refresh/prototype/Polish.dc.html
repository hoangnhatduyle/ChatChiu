What stays guarded

  • Every new colour pair passes the 4.5:1 contrast check before it can be committed.
  • The accessibility scan (axe) stays at zero findings on every screen, in light and dark.
  • Touch targets stay at least 44 px; keyboard and screen reader behaviour does not change.
  • New visual baselines are taken at six widths; the first-load JavaScript budget is unchanged because fonts and styles are not JavaScript.
